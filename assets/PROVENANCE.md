# Asset Library Provenance & Licensing

All textures, foliage cards, sky maps, and custom procedural meshes under `garden-source/public/assets/` and `garden-source/src/art/` are **100% original authored works** created specifically for this project (zero external hotlinks and zero third-party copyrighted assets).

- `textures/bark_albedo.webp`, `bark_normal.webp`, `bark_roughness.webp`: Original procedural cherry-bark PBR texture set.
- `textures/stone_albedo.webp`, `stone_normal.webp`, `stone_roughness.webp`: Original procedural weathered granite & moss PBR texture set.
- `textures/wood_albedo.webp`, `wood_normal.webp`: Original procedural timber plank grain & normal map.
- `textures/roof_tile_albedo.webp`, `roof_tile_normal.webp`: Original procedural kawara roof tile texture set.
- `textures/terrain_grass_albedo.webp`, `terrain_soil_albedo.webp`, `terrain_normal.webp`: Original procedural meadow & damp garden soil textures.
- `textures/water_normal.webp`: Original seamless multi-octave water ripple normal map.
- `textures/blossom_cluster.webp`, `shrub_foliage.webp`, `grass_wildflower_card.webp`, `waterlily_pad.webp`: Original hand-drawn Canvas foliage & blossom alpha cards.
- `textures/shoji_paper.webp`, `linen_cloth.webp`, `sky_twilight.webp`: Original procedural materials & twilight sky equirectangular map.
- `textures/memory_photo_art.webp`, `cinema_screen_art.webp`, `welcome_sign_art.webp`: Original non-copyrighted narrative artwork for Memory 01, 02, and 09.
