# Interaction references

- Bruno Simon: https://www.bruno-simon.com/ — discovery, immediate feedback, optional secrets. Used as an interaction principle, not copied art or 3D architecture.
- Ponpon Mania: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/ponpon-mania — awarded illustrated storytelling with chapter and mouse interactions. Inspired short, distinct responses within one visual world.
- GSAP matchMedia: https://gsap.com/docs/v3/GSAP/gsap.matchMedia()/ — responsive and reduced-motion considerations.

Implemented: optional lantern reveals original entrance message; firefly leaves a guiding light trail; playful ghost responds during horror chapter; blossom opens before original final note. Three small discovery markers track exploration. Native scrolling remains available throughout. Buttons have accessible labels, touch targets and keyboard activation. Reduced motion disables travelling particles and opens the note immediately.

No claim of award-winning quality or equivalence to these reference productions. Existing limited artwork separation remains unchanged.
